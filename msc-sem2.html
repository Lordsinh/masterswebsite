Semester II Curriculum & Drive Folders

Total Credits: 19.5 | 5 Core Theory Modules, 5 Practicals, and Minor Elective[cite: 1]

GLM 201 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Geomorphology & Geotectonics

[cite: 1]

Erosion cycles, morphometric analysis, neotectonics, paleomagnetism, mantle plumes, and Himalayan geodynamics.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 206 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Toposheet hypsometry, aerial photo geomorphology, and global plate tectonic feature maps.[cite: 1]

GLM 202 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Geochemistry

[cite: 1]

Biogeochemical cycles, trace element partitioning, REE patterns, stable isotopes, radiometric dating, and Eh-pH diagrams.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 207 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Rapid silicate analysis, mineral formula conversions, variation plots, and REE spidergrams.[cite: 1]

GLM 203 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Stratigraphy of India

[cite: 1]

Precambrian chronostratigraphy (Dharwar, Aravalli, Singhbhum), Marine Paleozoic, Mesozoic, and Cenozoic sequences.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 208 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Type locality specimen diagnostics, sequence stratigraphy correlation, and boundary analysis.[cite: 1]

GLM 204 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Micropaleontology & Oceanography

[cite: 1]

Foraminifera, nannofossils, radiolaria, conodonts, thermohaline circulation, CCD, and paleoclimatic reconstructions.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 209 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Microfossil separation, SEM morphology, paleobathymetry estimation, and palynomorph identification.[cite: 1]

GLM 205 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Ore Geology

[cite: 1]

Fluid inclusion dynamics, wall-rock alterations, orthomagmatic/sedimentary systems, and Indian metallic ore deposits.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 210 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Reflected light ore microscopy, microstructures, and Indian metallogenic maps.[cite: 1]

GLM 211M • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Earth System – Frontier Areas

[cite: 1]

Interdisciplinary minor course: Planetary origin, core convection, geomagnetism, continental drift, and ocean basins.[cite: 1]

Minor Elective Course[cite: 1]

Offered for Earth Science and inter-departmental postgraduate students.[cite: 1]
