Academic Scope

Core Geological Disciplines

Fundamental and applied pillars supporting geology studies and research

Mineralogy & Crystallography

Crystal systems, symmetry classes, refractive indices, pleochroism, and optical thin-section microscopy.

Petrology & Geochemistry

Igneous textures, metamorphic facies, sedimentary basin dynamics, and isotopic geochronology.

Stratigraphy & Paleontology

Chronostratigraphic correlations, biostratigraphy, microfossils, evolutionary trends, and mass extinctions.

Structural Geology & Tectonics

Stress-strain mechanics, shear zones, fold kinematics, plate boundary dynamics, and stereonet projections.

Remote Sensing & GIS

Satellite spectral bands, DEM terrain extraction, structural photogrammetry, and spatial GIS mapping.

Hydrogeology & Energy

Aquifer hydraulics, well hydraulics, coal petrology, kerogen kinetics, and petroleum systems.
