Semester I Curriculum & Drive Folders

Total Credits: 20.5 | 5 Core Theory Modules, 5 Practicals, and Geological Fieldwork[cite: 1]

GLM 101 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Structural Geology

[cite: 1]

2D stress/strain analysis, rock failure dynamics, buckling, ductile shear zones, mylonites, fabric analysis, and stereonets.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 106 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Geological cross-sections, stereonet fabric analysis, field data plotting, and dip-isogons.[cite: 1]

GLM 102 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Mineralogy & Crystallography

[cite: 1]

Silicate classifications (Neso-, Cyclo-, Ino-, Phyllo-, Tectosilicates), optical indicatrix, 32 classes, Bravais lattices, and EPMA/SEM.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 107 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Hand specimen identification, pleochroism schemes, plagioclase extinction, and goniometry.[cite: 1]

GLM 103 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Igneous Petrology

[cite: 1]

Magma physics, partial melting, AFC processes, binary/ternary equilibria (Ab-An, Di-Fo-Si), IUGS classification, and CIPW norms.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 108 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Megascopic/microscopic thin sections of igneous suites and CIPW norm calculations.[cite: 1]

GLM 104 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Sedimentology

[cite: 1]

Sandstone petrogenesis, Graywacke problem, dolomitization models, paleocurrent dynamics, facies architecture, and Quaternary systems.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 109 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

Sieve grain size analysis, cumulative curves, sedimentary structures, and environmental logs.[cite: 1]

GLM 105 • 2 Credits[cite: 1]

Metamorphic Petrology

[cite: 1]

Facies series, UHT/UHP metamorphism, Schreinemakers grids, anatexis, migmatites, P-T-t paths, and geothermobarometry.[cite: 1]

Practical: GLM 110 (1.5 Cr)[cite: 1]

ACF, AKF, AFM triangular calculations, reaction textures, and geothermobarometric modeling.[cite: 1]

GLM 111 • 3 Credits[cite: 1]

Geological Field Training

[cite: 1]

2 to 3 weeks of geological field mapping, rock specimen extraction, outcrop analysis, and final report submission.[cite: 1]

Field Evaluation (3 Cr)[cite: 1]

Field Report: 70 Marks | Field Viva-Voce: 30 Marks[cite: 1]
